Industry

My industrial experience spans new product development, current product engineering, and technology development, grounded in the rigorous use of numerical methods for design evaluation and virtual validation. I apply established simulation methodologies as well as develop new modelling approaches to assess safety, structural integrity, lifetime, and operational robustness of mechanical systems, supporting both the improvement of existing products and the development of new ones. High-fidelity numerical models are the backbone of this work, enabling the extraction of reliable engineering insight that reduces risk, supports design choices, and builds confidence in real operating and in-service conditions.
