Rafael Arturo Rubio Ruiz, PhD

Engineering Workflow Automation · Computational Mechanics · Materials Intelligence

I deliver engineering insight that informs product design, validation, and system-level understanding. I achieve this by designing and automating engineering workflows that integrate material modelling, numerical simulation, laboratory experiments, and high-quality field and system-level measurements. These include heterogeneous data such as thermal readings, pressures, vibration, deformation, and flow-related quantities, which are systematically integrated into coherent and validated high-fidelity engineering models. The workflows I develop are engineered to be systematic and reproducible, relying on robust scripting in Python and MATLAB combined with automation logic and agentic support that helps identify gaps, inconsistencies, and failure points, reducing fragility in complex automation pipelines rather than introducing it. My profile combines materials engineering, advanced applied mathematics, and computational mechanics, developed across both academic research and industrial R&D. I operate at the interface between models and people, communicating complex technical results clearly to both CAE and non-CAE audiences and ensuring that engineering insight is understood and used where decisions are made.

Industrial Competences

Simulation Workflow Automation

Design and deployment of automated CAE pipelines (Python, Abaqus, SimLab, Apex) that replace manual pre- and post-processing with standardized, repeatable simulation workflows.

Material Model Calibration & Inverse Methods

Calibration of fatigue, plasticity, and damage models using experimental data (extensometers, strain gauges, DIC, IRT, XRD) to ensure predictive reliability under real operating conditions, including extreme regimes such as impact and high temperature.

Static & Dynamic Numerical Analysis of Mechanical Systems

Numerical analysis of static, cyclic, and impact loading, contact mechanics, and thermo-mechanical coupling in metals and geomaterials.

CFD Analysis of Industrial Systems

Numerical analysis of fluid flows including multiphase, granular, reactive, and thermal phenomena.
